============================================================
  PVM MineStar Weekly Report Generator
  Capstone Copper — Engineer Self-Service App
============================================================

FIRST-TIME SETUP (do once)
---------------------------
1. Make sure Python 3.9+ is installed on your computer.

2. Open a terminal / command prompt in this folder and run:

      pip install -r requirements.txt

   If your corporate proxy blocks pip, ask IT to install:
      streamlit, pandas, python-pptx, openpyxl


RUNNING THE APP (every week)
-----------------------------
In a terminal / command prompt in this folder, run:

      streamlit run app.py

Your browser will open automatically at:
      http://localhost:8501


WHAT THE APP DOES
-----------------
1. You pick the Sunday of your reporting week.
   The app shows you the exact Mon–Sun date range to use.

2. You upload 5 CSV exports from Power BI (step-by-step instructions
   are shown in the app for each one).

3. Click "Generate PowerPoint Report".

4. Download the finished .pptx — already formatted in the
   Capstone Copper template, ready to present or send.

SUPPORT
-------
If a CSV fails to load, check that you exported it as .csv
(not .xlsx) from Power BI — see in-app instructions.
